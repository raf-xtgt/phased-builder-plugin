# Profile

## Initial Idea or Opportunity
The idea in their words, or "wants help finding one". If an opportunity is driving the project
(hackathon, incubator, VC pitch, launch), name it and link `opportunities/<name>/brief.md`.
> Used by `2-scope` and `7-opportunity`.

## Technical and Agent Experience
Coding background and AI-agent experience, kept separate. Brief evidence.
> Used by every skill to set explanation depth.

## Planning-Workflow Experience
New, familiar, or not established, with brief evidence. Separate from coding seniority.
> Used by every skill to set pacing.

## Learning Goals
What they want to get better at (tools, architecture, ways of working). Mentor-mode phases use this.
> Used by `4-spec` and `5-build` (mentor mode especially).

## Vocabulary and Concepts Likely to Need Explanation
Terms to explain, and terms they clearly know.

## Collaboration and Communication Preferences
Only what they stated: concise or guided; one question at a time or small batches.
Default: plain simple English, guided, one at a time.

## Default Mode Preference
vibe | mentor | decide per phase (default). Each phase still records its own mode.
