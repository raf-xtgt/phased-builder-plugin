# Social Launch (X, Discord, LinkedIn): Agent Reference

Goal: real users and real feedback, not vanity numbers. Read `research-rules.md` first.
The agent drafts; the user posts. No automated posting, bots, fake accounts, bought engagement, or
mass DMs: they break platform rules and the user's reputation.

## 1. Positioning (interview)
- Who exactly is it for, and where do those people already talk online?
- The one-liner and the one "aha" moment to show (from `PRODUCT.md` and `scope.md > The Unique Kernel`).
- What we want people to do: sign up, try it, join a waitlist, give feedback.
- How we'll know it worked: a small number of real signals (signups, replies, feedback messages).

## 2. Check current platform rules
Search for current rules before planning: community self-promotion rules for each Discord server or
subreddit-style community, platform policies on links and promotional content, and any recent changes.
Cite what you find. Don't state reach or algorithm claims as fact unless a current source supports them.

## 3. Assets
List what's needed and how to make each: 2–3 screenshots, a short screen-recorded demo or GIF of the aha
moment, a landing page or link that works on mobile, a feedback channel. Missing product pieces (e.g. a
landing page, a share link, analytics) can become a roadmap phase.

## 4. Per-platform plan
- **X:** short posts, the hook in the first line, one visual each. A launch thread: problem → demo GIF →
  what it does → link → ask for feedback. Build-in-public posts from real phases (`PHASE` and `BUG` log
  entries make good stories). Reply to people genuinely; join conversations where the audience already is.
- **LinkedIn:** a story or lesson framing ("what I learned building X"), professional tone, one image or
  short video, the link and the ask at the end. Good for B2B and for the user's own credibility.
- **Discord:** find servers where the target users are. Read each server's rules; many only allow
  promotion in specific channels or not at all. Give value first (answer questions, share learnings),
  ask a moderator if unsure. Consider the project's own server only once there are people to put in it.

## 5. Calendar
Write `opportunities/<name>/drafts/launch-plan.md`: a 2–4 week plan with dates, platform, post goal, draft
text, asset, and the metric to check. Keep it realistic for the user's hours per week.

## 6. Drafts and feedback loop
Drafts in `drafts/posts.md`, marked as drafts, in the user's voice (interview them; reuse their phrases).
Feedback from real users goes into `PARKING_LOT.md` (P-IDs) with the source, so it can become phases.
Log the launch as an `OPPORTUNITY` entry, and later results as a short follow-up entry.
